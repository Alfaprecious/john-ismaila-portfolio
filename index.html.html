COVID-19 Global Trends & Vaccination

Descriptive analysis of reported COVID-19 cases, deaths, country rates and vaccination coverage using the Our World in Data compact dataset.

Questions investigated

  • How did reported cases and deaths change over time?
  • Which countries ranked highest in cumulative case and death rates per million on a shared date?
  • How did reported vaccination and booster coverage vary across continents?
  • Did vaccination coverage and cumulative deaths appear associated in a fixed-date comparison?
19Retained source columns
5 pagesPower BI report/model
2020–26Source date coverage

Method & findings

  • Prepared fields in Power Query, standardised types, excluded aggregate geography rows to avoid double-counting, and preserved missing values as missing.
  • Built trend, country-ranking, continent-by-year and scatter-plot views; tested a country/date dimensional model.
  • Reported smoothed cases peaked at about 6.40 million on 26 Dec 2022, with China contributing about 5.88 million; smoothed deaths peaked earlier at about 14,821 on 28 Jan 2021.
  • On the 19 Feb 2023 snapshot, San Marino and Cyprus led reported cumulative case rates per million; Peru led reported cumulative death rates per million.
Limitations: Vaccination data are sparse and uneven. The fixed-date vaccination/death association is descriptive, not causal or an estimate of vaccine effectiveness. Country-day averages are not population-weighted.

E-Commerce Sales Analysis

SQL analysis of order, customer, employee and product data using a Northwind-style relational dataset.

Business questions

  • Which employees generated the most sales?
  • Who were the top five customers?
  • How did sales vary by month in 1997?
  • What products led their categories by revenue, and how did order quantities rank?
  • Which orders combined high total value with notable discounts?

Method & technical work

  • Joined related tables and used aggregates with GROUP BY, filters and HAVING.
  • Used CTEs, subqueries, conditional logic and date functions.
  • Practised product ranking with RANK() and order-quantity percentile ranking with PERCENT_RANK().
Results status: Query scope and techniques are documented, but final result tables and the complete SQL script were not available for this build. No sales totals or customer rankings are invented.

Air Crash Incident Analysis

Excel dashboard exploring recorded incidents, fatalities, manufacturers, operators and geography across the 1908–2024 project period.

Questions investigated

  • How did incident frequency change across years and decades?
  • Which years had the most recorded crashes and airborne fatalities?
  • Which named regions, manufacturers and operators appeared most frequently?
  • How did incidents and fatalities vary by month, and how did recent decades compare with earlier periods?

Method & findings

  • Cleaned text, standardised blanks, corrected date/numeric types, created a date field and flagged questionable values rather than guessing replacements.
  • Built summary tables, charts, KPI cards, slicers and a timeline.
  • 1946 had the highest recorded crash count (88 incidents); 1972 had the highest airborne fatality total (2,796 across 77 incidents).
  • Recorded crashes were lower in 2000–2024 (781) than 1975–1999 (1,466). Douglas appeared most often among manufacturers (990 records).
Limitations: This is an incident-record dataset, not accident risk per flight. Exposure data such as departures, flight hours or fleet size are absent. Some geography/operator fields are missing or questionable.

Working approach
